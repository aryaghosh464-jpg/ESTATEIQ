Research & Methodology Specification

The Mathematics of Property Intelligence

An inside look into the data curation, algorithmic modeling, and empirical audit behind ESTATEIQ's Kolkata valuation platform.

Curated Corpus

6,275 verified Kolkata residential property records systematically audited, deduplicated, and normalized across 40 continuous and categorical attributes.

Audited Integrity

Complete elimination of synthetic predictive leakage (such as target-derived luxury flags), establishing a defensible and honest predictive baseline.

L2 Regularization

Ridge Regression fitted on Log-transformed price space, minimizing multi-collinearity across 35 resident amenities and 308 micro-market encodings.

Chapter 01

Data Ingestion & Outlier Winsorization

Residential real estate data naturally suffers from positive skewness caused by trophy assets and penthouses. The raw Kolkata dataset exhibited a severe price skewness of 12.903.

Instead of deleting records—which discards market intelligence—ESTATEIQ implements an Interquartile Range (IQR) Winsorization treatment, clipping prices and areas to upper thresholds (1.5 × IQR). Furthermore, logarithmic transformation (Log(1 + Price)) compressed the skewness down to 0.271, yielding an approximately normal Gaussian target distribution ideal for linear and regularized estimators.

Chapter 02

Feature Engineering & Target Encoding

Real estate price is predominantly a spatial phenomenon. To represent 308 individual localities without exploding model dimensionality via one-hot encoding, we engineered:

  • Location Target Encoding: Encodes localities by their empirical historical mean price.
  • Location Tiers: Categorized into Low, Mid, and High valuation bands.
  • Bedroom Categorization: Grouped into Compact (≤2 BHK), Mid (3 BHK), and Large (≥4 BHK).
  • Amenity Score: Computed as non-zero presence across 35 individual lifestyle amenities.
Chapter 03

The End-to-End Leakage Audit

A cornerstone of our research integrity was the discovery and remediation of synthetic target leakage. In naive formulations, an engineered feature named IsLuxury had been created using the 70th percentile of property prices (correlation r = 0.847 with the target).

Empirical Honesty Over Artificial Benchmarks: While keeping IsLuxury yielded an apparent R² of 0.713, it was mathematically invalid because the model was predicting price using a feature derived from price itself. In our audited pipeline, this feature was completely removed. The honest baseline R² of 0.085 accurately reflects real-world portal limitations, where amenities carry virtually zero variance (99.1% rated 9).
